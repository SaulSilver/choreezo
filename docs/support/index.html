Privacy Policy

Choreezo is operated by Hatem Houssein in Barcelona, Spain.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Choreezo collects, why it is collected, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create or use a Choreezo account, we may collect:

  • Your email address
  • Your name or display name
  • Your authentication identifier
  • Information provided through Sign in with Apple or another authentication method

Choreezo does not receive your Apple ID password.

Household and chore information

To provide the app's features, we collect information you create or receive through Choreezo, including:

  • Apartment or household names
  • Household membership
  • Invitation codes
  • Chores and chore descriptions
  • Weekly schedules
  • Chore assignments and claims
  • Chore completion status
  • Reminder preferences

Some of this information is visible to other members of the household you join.

Device and notification information

When you enable notifications, we may collect:

  • Push notification tokens
  • Notification preferences
  • App installation identifiers
  • Basic device and app information required to deliver notifications

You can disable notifications through your device settings.

Technical information

We may automatically receive limited technical information necessary to operate and secure the app, such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • IP address
  • Authentication and security logs
  • Error and diagnostic information

Support communications

When you contact us, we collect the information contained in your message and any contact details you provide.

2. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Allow you to create or join a household
  • Display and synchronize chores between household members
  • Assign, claim, reassign, and complete chores
  • Send reminders and app notifications
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the app
  • Prevent misuse and unauthorized access
  • Respond to support requests
  • Diagnose technical problems
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information for targeted advertising.

3. Legal basis for processing

Where European or United Kingdom data protection law applies, we process information under one or more of the following legal bases:

  • To provide the Choreezo service you requested
  • To pursue legitimate interests, such as securing and improving the app
  • With your consent, such as when you enable notifications
  • To comply with legal obligations

You may withdraw consent for optional processing at any time, without affecting processing that occurred before the withdrawal.

4. How information is shared

Other household members

Your display name, household membership, assigned chores, claimed chores, and completion status may be visible to other users who belong to the same household.

Do not join a household or use an invitation code unless you trust the people who have access to it.

Service providers

Choreezo uses service providers that process information on our behalf, including Google Firebase for authentication, database storage, hosting, notifications, and related infrastructure.

These providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international data transfers.

Legal requirements

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or a valid legal request
  • Protect the safety, security, and rights of Choreezo or its users
  • Investigate fraud, misuse, or security incidents

5. Selling and advertising

Choreezo does not sell or rent your personal information.

Choreezo does not share your personal information for cross-app targeted advertising.

6. Data retention

We retain account and household information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service.

When you delete your account, we delete or anonymize personal information associated with it, except where:

  • Retention is required by law
  • Limited information is required for security, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution
  • Shared household records must remain available to other household members

Information retained in backups may remain for a limited period before being removed through the normal backup deletion process.

7. Account and data deletion

You can initiate deletion of your Choreezo account from the account settings inside the app.

Deleting your account will permanently remove your account and associated personal information, subject to the limited retention situations described above.

We may need to verify your identity before completing a privacy request.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive a portable copy of certain information
  • Withdraw consent
  • Submit a complaint to your local data protection authority

To exercise these rights, contact john.wilson011019@gmail.com.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet-based service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Children's privacy

Choreezo is designed for people coordinating responsibilities in a shared household. It is not designed for use by children without the involvement and supervision of a parent or legal guardian.

Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information improperly.

11. Third-party services

Choreezo may interact with services provided by Apple, Google, and Firebase. Those companies may process information under their own terms and privacy policies when you use their services.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Choreezo's features, legal obligations, or data practices change.

The effective date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated. Material changes may also be communicated through the app.
